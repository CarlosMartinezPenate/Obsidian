# Alcian Blue Staining Solution

## Purpose

Preparation of **1% (w/v) Alcian Blue** in **3% (v/v) acetic acid** for staining acidic extracellular polysaccharides (EPS).

This solution is suitable for qualitative microscopy of live algal cultures.

---

# Reagents

- Alcian Blue 8GX powder
- Glacial acetic acid
- Ultrapure or distilled water

---

# Formula

## 3% Acetic Acid

For any final volume:

```text
Glacial acetic acid (mL) = Final volume × 0.03

Water (mL) = Final volume − Acetic acid
```

---

## Alcian Blue

```text
Alcian Blue (g) = Final volume (mL) × 0.01
```

# Preparation

## Step 1

Prepare the required volume of **3% acetic acid**.

Always add:

> **Acid → Water**

Never water into concentrated acid.

---

## Step 2

Add the required amount of Alcian Blue powder.

---

## Step 3

Mix until completely dissolved.

Gentle stirring is preferred.

Avoid vigorous shaking that introduces bubbles.

---

## Step 4

(Optional)

Filter through a **0.45 µm** membrane if particulate material is present.

This improves microscopy by reducing dye particles.

---

## Step 5

Transfer to an amber bottle or wrap the container in aluminum foil.

Label with:

- Reagent name
- Concentration
- Preparation date
- Initials

---

# Storage

Store:

- Room temperature
- Protected from light

Discard if:

- visible precipitation develops
- contamination is suspected
- color changes markedly

---

# Safety

> [!warning]
> Alcian Blue solution contains acetic acid.

Wear:

- Laboratory coat
- Gloves
- Eye protection

Avoid:

- inhalation of powder
- skin contact
- eye contact

Dispose of waste according to institutional chemical-waste procedures.

---

# Notes

- Working pH should be approximately **2.5**.
- This formulation preferentially stains **acidic polysaccharides**, including sulfated and carboxylated extracellular polymers.
- It is intended for **qualitative microscopy**, not quantitative EPS determination.
- A sterile **F/2 + Alcian Blue** control should always be prepared to assess background staining or dye precipitation.
