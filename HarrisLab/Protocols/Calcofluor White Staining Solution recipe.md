# Calcofluor White Staining Solution

## Purpose

Calcofluor White binds **β-linked polysaccharides**, including:

- Cellulose
- Chitin
- Cellulosic thecal plates

In dinoflagellates, it is commonly used to visualize the cellulose-containing theca.

---

# Reagents

- Calcofluor White powder (Fluorescent Brightener 28)
- Sterile distilled water

---

# Stock solution

Prepare:

**1 mg/mL**

Example:

| Final volume | Calcofluor White |
|--------------|-----------------:|
| 10 mL | 10 mg |
| 25 mL | 25 mg |
| 50 mL | 50 mg |
| 100 mL | 100 mg |

Dissolve in distilled water.

Mix until fully dissolved.

Filter sterilization is optional.

---

# Storage

Store:

- 4°C
- Protected from light

Stable for several months.

---

# Working solution

Recommended final concentration:

**10–50 µg/mL**

For routine microscopy:

**25 µg/mL** is a good starting point.

---

# Staining protocol

1. Pipette approximately **100 µL** of live culture into a microcentrifuge tube.

2. Add Calcofluor White to a final concentration of **25 µg/mL**.

3. Mix gently.

4. Incubate for **5–15 minutes** at room temperature.

5. Mount **10–20 µL** on a microscope slide.

6. Image immediately.

---

# Microscopy

Image using:

- UV excitation (preferred)
or
- 405 nm laser if available

Expected emission:

Blue fluorescence.

Acquire:

- Brightfield
- Calcofluor fluorescence
- Chlorophyll autofluorescence

---

# Expected observations

## Intact cells

Strong fluorescent outline of the thecal plates.

---

## Shed thecal material

Bright fluorescent fragments.

Empty fluorescent shells.

Detached plates.

---

## Extracellular EPS

Usually little or no staining.

---

# Interpretation

| Observation | Interpretation |
|-------------|----------------|
| Cell outline fluoresces | Intact cellulose theca |
| Empty fluorescent shell | Shed theca |
| Small fluorescent fragments | Plate debris |
| Diffuse non-fluorescent fog | Probably EPS rather than cellulose |
| Diffuse fluorescent matrix | Cellulose-containing extracellular material (less common) |

---

# Safety

Wear gloves and eye protection.

Protect the solution from light.

Dispose of waste according to institutional guidelines.

---

# Notes

Calcofluor White is complementary to Alcian Blue.

- **Alcian Blue** → acidic extracellular polysaccharides (EPS)
- **Calcofluor White** → cellulose-containing structures (thecal plates)

Using both stains on parallel samples can help distinguish extracellular mucus from shed thecal material.
