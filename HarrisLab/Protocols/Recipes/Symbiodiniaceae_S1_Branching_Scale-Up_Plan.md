# Symbiodiniaceae S1 Branching, Scale-Up, Plating, and Archive Plan

## Objective

Branch each established **S1 F/2 culture** into:

1. **M1** — a fresh maintenance backup;
2. **S2** — a biomass-production culture;
3. **F2P1** — low-density F/2 agar plates for colony recovery and solid-phase backup;
4. **A1** — frozen analytical archive pellets;
5. **H1** — a first pilot harvest from **SSB01** only.

Each branch is derived directly from the original S1 culture:

```text
S1
├──► M1
├──► S2
├──► F2P1
├──► A1
└──► H1 (SSB01 only)
```

> [!important]
> Establish **M1 before harvesting or archiving the source culture**.
>
> Keep the residual S1 cultures as temporary emergency backups until M1 and S2 are clearly established.
>
> Do not use bacterial glycerol-stock conditions for these dinoflagellates. A1 is a **non-viable analytical pellet archive**, not validated cryopreservation.

# Low-density F/2 agar plating

The purpose of F2P1 is to establish a **solid-phase colony-recovery backup**, not to prove axenicity.

## Plate layout

Prepare two plates per strain:

| Plate code | Inoculum |
|---|---|
| F2P1-10-1 | 100 µL of 10⁻¹ dilution |
| F2P1-10-2 | 100 µL of 10⁻² dilution |

For five strains:

- 5 strains × 2 plates = **10 inoculated plates**
- 2 uninoculated F/2 agar controls = **2 plates**
- **Total = 12 plates**

> [!note]
> Because these cultures are strongly clumped, some colonies may originate from small aggregates rather than single cells.

## Incubation conditions

- Temperature: **24–26°C**
- Photoperiod: **12 h light : 12 h dark**
- Light: approximately **20–30 µmol photons m⁻² s⁻¹**
- Protect plates from drying
- Inspect weekly
- Expected colony-development period: approximately **2–4 weeks**, possibly longer

## Follow-up after colony formation

1. Identify discrete pigmented colonies.
2. Pick one colony into a small fresh liquid F/2 culture.
3. Transfer part of the same colony to a fresh F/2 agar plate.
4. Keep the original plate until both daughter cultures establish.

The resulting lineage is:

```text
F2P1 colony
├──► liquid colony-derived backup
└──► fresh solid F/2 backup
```

# Pre-work preparation

## One or more days before inoculation

1. Confirm that all S1 cultures are alive by microscopy.
2. Record:
   - OD750;
   - culture color;
   - visible clumping or sediment;
   - chlorophyll autofluorescence, when available;
   - approximate source volume.
3. Clean the required glassware according to [[Cleaning Glassware]].
4. Prepare gauze closures.
5. Sterilize dry flasks and closures according to [[Autoclaving Protocol]].
6. Prepare sterile ASW according to [[ASW recipe]].
7. Prepare 4.0 L fresh F/2 according to [[F2 Medium recipe]].
8. Prepare and pour 12 low-density F/2 agar plates.
9. Label all flasks, plates, dilution tubes, archive tubes, and harvest tubes before beginning culture transfers.
10. Confirm incubator space for:
    - 5 M1 flasks;
    - 5 S2 flasks;
    - 5 remaining S1 source flasks;
    - 12 F/2 agar plates.

# Aseptic branching procedure

## 1. Arrange the work

Process one strain at a time in this order:

```text
SSA01 → SSA02 → SSA03 → SSB01 → SSE01
```

For each strain, perform the handling steps in this order:

```text
M1 → S2 → F2P1 → A1 → retain S1
```

Use a new sterile pipette for each strain and for each transfer type when cross-contamination is possible.

---

## 2. Mix the S1 source culture

1. Inspect the source culture before opening.
2. Gently swirl or invert as appropriate to distribute cells.
3. Do not vortex.
4. For strongly clumped SSB01, mix consistently but avoid aggressive mechanical disruption unless the dispersed-versus-clumped condition is being tested deliberately.
5. Remove a small microscopy sample after mixing.

---

## 3. Establish M1

For every strain:

1. Add **80 mL** fresh F/2 to a sterile 250 mL flask.
2. Add **20 mL** of the corresponding S1 culture.
3. Mix gently by swirling.
4. Close with a sterile breathable closure.
5. Return the flask to the incubator.

---

## 4. Establish S2

### SSA01 and SSE01

1. Add 400 mL fresh F/2 to one labeled 2 L flask.
2. Add 100 mL of the corresponding S1 culture.
3. Mix gently.

### SSA02, SSA03, and SSB01

For each strain:

1. Add 900 mL fresh F/2 to one labeled 2 L flask.
2. Add 100 mL S1 inoculum.
3. Mix gently.

---

## 5. Prepare F2P1 plates

1. Mix the source culture thoroughly but gently.
2. Prepare 10⁻¹ and 10⁻² dilutions using sterile F/2 or sterile ASW.
3. Plate 100 µL of the 10⁻¹ dilution onto **F2P1-10-1**.
4. Plate 100 µL of the 10⁻² dilution onto **F2P1-10-2**.
5. Prepare **one shared pair of uninoculated control plates for the complete plating batch**.
6. Allow the inoculum to absorb completely.
7. Incubate under the conditions described below.
8. Record the inoculation date and plate IDs.

---

## 6. Prepare A1 analytical pellet archives

For each strain:

1. Divide **15 mL total culture** into three labeled 15 mL centrifuge tubes:
   - 5 mL per replicate;
   - three replicates per strain.
2. Centrifuge using a validated setting that pellets the cells without unnecessary damage.
3. Remove the supernatant carefully.
4. Either:
   - freeze the pellet directly in the centrifuge tube, if the tube is approved for −80°C storage; or
   - transfer the pellet into a labeled cryovial using the laboratory-approved method.
5. Record the culture volume represented by each pellet.
6. Flash-freeze if this is the laboratory-approved practice.
7. Store at −80°C.
8. Record the freezer, rack, box, and position.

---

## 7. Retain residual S1 cultures

The residual S1 cultures serve as emergency backups.

1. Leave each S1 culture in its original flask whenever possible.
2. Do not routinely add fresh medium.
3. Label the flask as **S1-BACKUP** if it must be transferred.
4. Retain for approximately **2–4 weeks**.
5. Retire, archive, or harvest S1 after:
	   - M1 is established;
	   - S2 is actively growing;
	   - no major contamination or transfer failure is evident.
6. F2P1 colony recovery may continue independently after S1 retirement.

For **SSB01**, the approximately **80 mL** remaining after M1 and S2 establishment may be allocated as follows:

- 5 mL for microscopy, OD750, and plate preparation;
- 3 × 5 mL for A1 analytical pellets;
- approximately 50 mL for the H1 pilot harvest;
- approximately 10 mL retained temporarily as an S1 backup.

> [!caution]
> Measure the actual S1 volume before beginning. If any source culture contains less than approximately **150 mL**, establish M1 first and reduce or postpone that strain's S2 scale-up rather than consuming the entire source.

---

## 8. Pilot harvest of SSB01

Only begin after **SSB01-M1** and **SSB01-S2** have been established.

Assuming approximately **80 mL SSB01-S1** remains:
- 5 mL for microscopy, OD750, and plate preparation;
- 3 × 5 mL for A1 analytical pellets;
- approximately 50 mL for H1;
- approximately 10 mL retained temporarily as S1 backup.

1. Measure the remaining SSB01-S1 volume.
2. Preserve a small residual SSB01-S1 backup if practical.
3. Record:
   - OD750;
   - microscopy;
   - pigmentation;
   - clumping;
   - overall culture appearance.
4. Divide the harvest volume among 50 mL tubes.
5. Centrifuge using the validated harvest condition.
6. Record:
   - total culture volume harvested;
   - number of tubes;
   - pellet appearance;
   - wet pellet mass, if measured.
7. Allocate pellets according to the downstream plan:
   - fresh extraction;
   - flash-frozen protein/pigment pellet;
   - DNA archive.
8. Store immediately at −80°C or proceed directly with downstream processing.

# Incubation

## Liquid cultures

Maintain the new liquid cultures under the current validated conditions unless a deliberate variable is being tested.

- Temperature: **26°C**
- Photoperiod: **12 h light : 12 h dark**
- Light intensity at flask level: approximately **40 µmol photons m⁻² s⁻¹**
- Closures: sterile breathable gauze
- Maintain adequate flask spacing and headspace.

Record the actual light intensity across incubator positions with the chamber closed.

## F2P1 plates

- Temperature: **24–26°C**
- Photoperiod: **12 h light : 12 h dark**
- Light intensity: approximately **20–30 µmol photons m⁻² s⁻¹**
- Protect plates from drying.
- Inspect without repeatedly opening plates.

# Acceptance criteria

## M1 success

An M1 culture is accepted as the maintenance replacement when:

- cells remain visibly intact;
- chlorophyll pigmentation is retained;
- microscopy shows living cells;
- no obvious contamination is detected;
- cell abundance or pigmentation is stable or increasing.

Do **not** discard the corresponding S1 culture until successful M1 establishment has been confirmed.

## S2 success

An S2 culture is considered suitable for continued biomass production when:

- pigmentation is retained after transfer;
- microscopy supports active growth;
- no obvious contamination is present;
- culture density or visible biomass increases over time.

## F2P1 colony recovery

Successful plating is indicated by:

- discrete pigmented colonies;
- minimal contamination;
- colonies that can be transferred into fresh liquid F/2.

If no colonies develop after several weeks, reassess:

- inoculum density;
- agar preparation;
- plate moisture;
- illumination;
- incubation temperature.

Only then consider Marine Broth contamination screening.

# Notes

- M1 is the priority branch and should always be established first.
- S1 cultures should be retained until M1 and S2 have clearly recovered.
- F2P1 is intended to generate colony-derived backups independent of the liquid cultures; it should not be considered a successful backup until colonies have formed and been transferred.

- H1 is reserved for the initial SSB01 biomass harvest and downstream extraction tests.
