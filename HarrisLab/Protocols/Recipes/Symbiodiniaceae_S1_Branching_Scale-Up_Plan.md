``` text
S1 → M1 → S2 → F2P1 → A1 → H1 (SSB01 only)
```

-   Fresh F/2: **4.0 L**

## S1 balance

Each S1 (\~200 mL):

-   20 mL → M1
-   100 mL → S2
-   \~80 mL retained as temporary S1 backup

Keep S1 until: - M1 established - S2 growing - F2P1 evaluated

## F2P1

For each strain:

-   Plate 100 µL of 10⁻¹
-   Plate 100 µL of 10⁻²

Controls:

-   2 uninoculated F/2 agar plates

Incubation:

-   24--26°C
-   12:12
-   20--30 µmol photons m⁻² s⁻¹

## A1

Prepare three analytical archive pellets per strain.

## H1

Harvest remaining SSB01 only after M1 and S2 have been established.
