Hu et al. purified **NblA** and **NblB** as recombinant proteins in two tagged formats:
1. **His₆-tagged NblA/NblB**
2. **GST-tagged NblA/NblB**
---
## **1. His₆-tagged NblA/NblB**

### **Expression conditions**

- **Host:** _E. coli_ BL21(DE3)
- **Medium:** LB
- **Antibiotic:** kanamycin, 20 µg/mL
- **Temperature:** 16°C
- **Induction:** IPTG, 1 mM
- **Post-induction growth:** 12 h

### **Cell resuspension and lysis buffer**
Cell pellets containing His₆-tagged proteins were resuspended in ice-cold potassium phosphate buffer:

- **20 mM KPB, pH 7.2**
- **0.5 M NaCl**
- **ice-cold conditions**

### **Cell disruption**

- **Method:** French press
- **Pressure:** 1200 bar
- **Condition:** chilled
- **Passages:** several

### **Clarification**

After lysis, the suspension was centrifuged:

- **12,000g**
- **40 min**
- **4°C**

The supernatant contained the crude soluble proteins and was used for purification.

### **Purification**

The His₆-tagged proteins were purified by **Ni²⁺-affinity chromatography**
#### **Elution buffer**

The His₆-tagged proteins were eluted using:

- **20 mM KPB, pH 7.2**
- **0.5 M NaCl**
- **0.5 M imidazole**

#### **Dialysis**

After Ni²⁺-affinity purification, proteins were dialyzed against the appropriate potassium phosphate buffer needed for the reactions. For His-tagged proteins used in the protein-interaction section, the dialysis buffer is specified as:

- **20 mM KPB, pH 7.2**

## **Practical workflow**

His₆-NblA/NblB  
→ expressed in _E. coli_ BL21(DE3)  
→ grown in LB + kanamycin  
→ induced at 16°C with 1 mM IPTG for 12 h  
→ lysed in 20 mM KPB pH 7.2 + 0.5 M NaCl  
→ disrupted by French press at 1200 bar  
→ centrifuged at 12,000g for 40 min at 4°C  
→ purified by Ni²⁺-affinity chromatography  
→ eluted with 20 mM KPB pH 7.2 + 0.5 M NaCl + 0.5 M imidazole  
→ dialyzed into reaction-appropriate KPB (20 mM KPB, pH 7.2)

## **2. GST-tagged NblA/NblB**

These came from cloning **nblA** and **nblB** into **pGEX-4T-1**.
### **Purification**

GST-tagged proteins were purified by: **GST affinity chromatography**
### **Elution buffer**

The GST-tagged proteins were eluted using:

- **50 mM Tris-HCl, pH 8.0**
- **10 mM reduced glutathione**

Reduced glutathione releases GST-fusion proteins from glutathione resin.
### **Dialysis buffer**

After GST purification, proteins were dialyzed against buffer A:

- **10 mM Na₂HPO₄**
- **1.8 mM KH₂PO₄**
- **140 mM NaCl**
- **2.7 mM KCl**
- **pH 8.0**
## **Practical workflow**

GST-NblA/NblB  
→ purified by GST affinity chromatography  
→ eluted with 50 mM Tris-HCl pH 8.0 + 10 mM reduced glutathione  
→ dialyzed into buffer A  
→ used as bait in GST pull-down assays

Buffer A:
- 10 mM Na₂HPO₄
- 1.8 mM KH₂PO₄
- 140 mM NaCl
- 2.7 mM KCl
- pH 8.0
